Pip Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Pip Studio is a personal content tool operated by Adriel Reyes. It publishes original videos to the operator's own TikTok account, @HeyPip.

What we access

When the operator connects TikTok, Pip Studio reads basic profile information (display name, username and avatar) through the user.info.basic scope, and uploads or publishes videos through the video.upload and video.publish scopes.

How it is stored

The access token is stored only on the operator's own computer. It is not sent to any third party.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or disclose any data. Pip Studio has no other users and collects no data from viewers or visitors.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services.
